# Annora Explorer product context

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Buyers inspect an industrial product and explain requirements in context. Sellers
prepare product explanations, review inquiries and issue reviewed sample quotes.
Patrick and Wesley present the same buyer/seller journey to prospective customers.
Developers and agents integrate prepared model packages with existing websites.

## Product Purpose

Connect product understanding to a precise request and a traceable next action.
Support both Annora's Explorer and clients that already have a viewer. Reduce the
manual work required to prepare another product without weakening review gates.

## Operating Context

Explorer is the inspection and explanation surface; Product Room is the buyer
workspace; Studio is the seller workspace. They share model, source drawing,
association, overview and commercial-record identities. A customer presentation
uses the same project and explicit confirmation boundaries as ordinary use.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current baseline is recorded in docs/explorer-to-quote-progress.md. This is a
public static template with fictional, browser-local records. No backend,
credentials, real customer database or external delivery is authorized here.
Client content remains outside the public starter. Hosts own their repositories.
Historical inquiries and issued quotations retain their exact references.
Prepared motion explains an authored mechanism; geometry alone does not establish
engineering behavior. Ordinary inspection capture is supported; arbitrary active
tour or spatial-presentation capture is not.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the approved compact floating interface, model prominence, Engineering and
Atlas-informed linework, configurable accent with Ember default, Light/Dark and
existing controls. All maintained copy and documentation are English.

## Product Principles

- Explain a relationship with short actions and relevant technical views.
- Carry the selected context into requests without silently changing it.
- Reduce navigation and repeated entry while keeping explicit review.
- Preserve exact versions and ownership of the next action.
- Reuse configuration, prepared assets and one existing player/render loop.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Mobile web, keyboard access and ordinary-computer performance are requirements.
No required action may depend on hover. Keep pause, replay, seek and manual takeover.
Physical-device performance and human comprehension require their own evidence.
