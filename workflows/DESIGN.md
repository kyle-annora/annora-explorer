## Composition and behavior

A compact header offers one live-walkthrough action. A concise title introduces
one connected three-chapter map. Sequential numbering communicates order. Buyer
and seller are named on every step; gate labels remain visible while explanations
expand locally. A direct v1-to-next-step note keeps the sample revision from being
mistaken for a compulsory business step.

Five branch maps sit below in native disclosures, closed initially. An expanded
branch contains a directed step sequence, decision rules and a relevant example.
Hash links select/open a branch or a main step for sharing. Opening another branch
closes the prior one. Keyboard and touch never depend on hover. Mobile stacks the
chapters and keeps each explanation next to its trigger.

Tokens: canvas #f3f3f1, surface #ffffff, ink #282b2d, muted #65696d, border #d7d8d7,
accent #ff6840 (marks only, not small text). Primary CTA is graphite with white type.
Body 16px; secondary 14px; metadata 12px minimum. Controls at least 44 px. 4–6px
control radii. No shadow, external font requests, photography or WebGL canvas.
The flow diagram is the visual artifact; there is no decorative hero graphic.
